[![Reward](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261118/danikluue-max/makefun-news-image-20261118/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261118)

One image that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026: 48 verified stories, 6
continents, one day. A dark navy infographic with a huge serif date, hero
numbers, a timeline of how the day unfolded, 6 big lead cards with headlines
built to read at 300px wide, a real Natural Earth world map with the 12
stories pinned by latitude and longitude, field bars, and a 42-item list of
the rest of the day.

- `main.ts` — the entry program: copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
- `page.html` — the page the image was rendered from (HTML + inline SVG).
- `fonts/PlayfairDisplay.ttf` — the newspaper serif, SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (see `fonts/OFL.txt`).
